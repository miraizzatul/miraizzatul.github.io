MIRA IZZATUL - GAME DEVELOPER

Abiotic Factor
Jul 2022 - Aug 2025


Abiotic Factor is an open-world multiplayer survival crafting game up to six players set within a sprawling underground research facility. After a catastrophic containment failure, players explore interconnected sectors, gather resources, build elaborate bases, and work together to survive against paranormal threats while uncovering the secrets of the facility.

As a Gameplay Programmer at Deep Field Games, I contributed to a wide range of gameplay systems throughout the development of Abiotic Factor leading up to its 1.0 release. Working closely with designers, artists, and fellow programmers, my role involved implementing new gameplay features, expanding core systems, improving multiplayer functionality, and ensuring new content integrated seamlessly into a large and evolving codebase.


I personally

  • Developed the core gameplay architecture for the Cooking and Pet systems, supporting world placement, interaction, persistence, and designer extensibility.

  • Investigated and integrated Unreal Engine's Push Model Networking, migrating existing C++ replicated systems to improve multiplayer replication performance and maintainability.

  • Expanded the save system to support the game's open-world structure, including persistent world modifications, player-owned furniture, resource states, and individual player progression.

  • Implemented new player mechanics including Jetpack movement, Combat Roll, and expanded interactions such as applying medical items and offering food to other players.

  • Developed melee and projectile combat systems, including support for unique weapons such as the Vacuum and Net Launcher, along with their bespoke gameplay behaviors.

  • Collaborated closely with designers to implement new Perks, Buffs, and progression mechanics that dynamically altered player and creature gameplay based on skills and character progression.

  • Worked extensively with UMG, implementing and iterating on the Player HUD, Inventory, and Crafting interfaces while incorporating designer direction and player feedback.

  • Contributed gameplay improvements, bug fixes, and technical polish across numerous gameplay systems to help deliver a stable and polished 1.0 release.


Working on Abiotic Factor gave me the opportunity to contribute to both large-scale gameplay features and the underlying systems that support a modern multiplayer survival game. From implementing player-facing mechanics to improving networking, persistence, UI, and gameplay architecture, every feature required balancing technical design, maintainability, and player experience. Shipping the game through its full 1.0 release was an incredibly rewarding experience and significantly strengthened my skills as a gameplay programmer.

Abiotic Factor has received widespread critical acclaim and industry recognition, winning the Grand Prize and Excellence in Design at the 2024 NZ Game Awards (The Pavs) . The game was also nominated for Best Multiplayer Game at the 2024 Golden Joystick Awards , Excellence in Narrative and Excellence in Accessibility at the 2024 NZ Game Awards, and Best Indie Game and PC Game of the Year at the 2025 Golden Joystick Awards.
